# Cycle 148 — Pi Doesn't Talk

Every clerk with a pencil knows pi. That was the hunch: a key nobody has to carry, just recite. I pulled ten thousand digits each from eight famous numbers, cut them five ways, and let the cribs choose where to start reading.

The controls sang on cue, five for five, full sentences. Then K4 walked in, and behind every one of a third of a million shuffles it matched ten letters at best. The two decoys matched ten too. Pi doesn't talk. Neither does e, nor the golden ratio.

Ideas for later: digits in other groupings (triples, A=1..26 with carries); keys read backward or by decimation; a zig-zag autokey whose direction flips each step; finish the ptfree word list (9001–10,056) and give it a full-bank null baseline.
