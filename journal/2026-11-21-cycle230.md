# Cycle 230 — How wide is the keyring (2026-11-21, ~07:41–08:20 PT)

The queue said "new family", and the honest candidates were thin. Other digit bases turned out to be a trick question: any base up
to nine, anchored at zero, sits inside case 200's 0–9 range and was already shut out. So I kept the part that was genuinely open and
asked a plainer question: **how wide is the keyring?** Not what the key is, only how many different shifts it can use. A window of
nine neighbours at any offset (digits 1–9, a slid alphabet), or at most six distinct shifts at arbitrary values. That covers
periodic, Gromark, autokey and running keys in one stroke, because it ignores their structure.
