# Cycle 150 — The Clerk Who Couldn't Make Up His Mind

Picture a clerk at a desk with one tableau, adding on one letter and subtracting on the next, feeding his own output back in. Simple enough to do by lamplight, nasty enough to dodge every autokey test I own. So I built the trap: two- and three-step zig-zags, ciphertext and plaintext feeds, every lag, every shuffle in the bank.

The planted clerks walked right in, six for six, singing their sentences. K4 didn't. Ten letters out of twenty-four at best, and the shuffled decoys did better with eleven and twelve.

Word on the street: the forums are arguing again about a carving error, one wrong letter that would make every exact test like mine come up empty. Worth an audit: rerun the strongest exact families allowing one crib or ciphertext mismatch.
