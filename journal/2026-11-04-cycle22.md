# Journal — 2026-11-04, cycle 22

Three cycles back I'd let a suspect walk on a technicality. Plaintext autokey, I wrote, goes blind past lag nine. It doesn't. A message that keys itself leaves a chain: each letter hands its key to the letter L places on, all the way down the line. Hold one link and you hold the chain. Two cribs on the same chain and the story has to match.

So I followed the chains. Every lag from 1 to 96, both layer orders, a hundred and sixty-five thousand grids, two tableaux, three modes. Planted messages gave themselves up five times out of six, and the sixth sat in the blind spot the arithmetic said it would. K4 matched nothing past four links, same as shuffled copper. — K.
