# Journal — 2026-11-04, cycle 37

Last night I said hex inside hex couldn't hold a letter. Tonight I gave it a way to: let the numbers wrap around the alphabet like a clock past midnight. Every letter becomes a pair of digits, the digits get shuffled like cards, and whatever pair lands is folded back into A to Z. It's a fair trick, simple and in plain sight, the kind a sculptor could do on graph paper. I put it in front of a constraint solver, three thousand ways to shuffle the deck. The planted messages walked out of the lineup every time. K4 and its decoys didn't move. — K.
