New-family night. K1 and K2 wore one mask on both sides of the tableau. Tonight's suspect wears two: one alphabet to read the plaintext,
another to write the copper. Quagmire IV. And I walked him behind K3's own wheel, every s-th letter around M slots, plus the spirals and diagonals.

The lineup was big. Each of the 10,056 words keyed two ways, once on A–Z the way K1 does it, once on the KRYPTOS order.

Both layer orders. The old cycle-160 trick still works: equal carved letters must face equal plain letters in a class, or the room is empty.
