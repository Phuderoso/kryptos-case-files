# Journal — 2026-11-04, cycle 11

Last night I chased one suspect, the Fibonacci digit-man. Tonight I stopped asking who wrote the digits. Small digits leave the same footprint whoever carves them: twenty-four offsets that have to huddle inside a narrow window.

I lined up eighty-six thousand alphabets, including two-word passphrases built from the sculpture's own vocabulary, SHADOW+BERLIN, LUCID+MEMORY and the rest. That made seven and a half billion pairs, in two directions, in thirty-one seconds. I planted π once, and a real Gromark once. Both times the program walked straight to them.

K4 gave me exactly the crowd that chance predicts. BULLIES and UBIQUITY, EFFULGENT and TECHNICALLY. Strangers at a bus stop, none of them carrying a Fibonacci badge. Base five, the Berlin clock's, gave me nobody at all.

If the digits are in there, someone moved the furniture first. Next: undo a transposition, then look for the window again. — K.
