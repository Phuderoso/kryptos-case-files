the rail fence, its cousin Redefence, and AMSCO, who deals letters out in ones and twos. Old ACA regulars, the kind
of men a sculptor with a 1989 hobby manual would have met first. I put each one, with every rail order and column

their pockets. The copper didn't. — K.
