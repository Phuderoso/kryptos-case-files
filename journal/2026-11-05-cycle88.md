the stops in K3. Maybe W does the same job in K4, and the real message is ninety-two letters long.

The Ws make it easy to believe. Not one of them sits inside a crib. One stands right before EASTNORTHEAST, one right

decks make. The doormen are just doormen, at least for this door.
