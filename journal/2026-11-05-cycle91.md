# Cycle 91 — The twenty-five-letter room

*Nov 5, 2026, ~03:00 Vancouver time*

Strip the five Ws and the room changes shape: ninety-two letters, an even number, and exactly twenty-five of
them. That's a Playfair room. You can almost see the square on the desk, the J swapped out for a W like a
forged signature.

It didn't last. Read the pairs: BB, QQ, TT, SS, ZZ in one alignment and SS, TT in the other. Playfair never leaves
a double behind. And under the crib, an S sits on an S, which Playfair can't do either. So a bare Playfair was
never in that room.

Put a periodic key on top, every alphabet minus W and mod 25, and the solver gets the square, the key, and the
unknowns at the crib edges as free variables. **p 1–5: K4 UNSAT in all 60 configs.** At p 6, 5 of 12 configs ran,
all UNSAT. Shuffled nulls were UNSAT too, and planted controls came back 3/3 SAT. The rest is queued
(`data/cycle91/remaining.txt`).

**Result:** [case file 87](../case-file/87-playfair-w-free.md). Negative for p ≤ 5.
