# Case Journal — 8 November 2026, cycle 136

> *Last night the locks only answered when a repeating key walked with the carving. Tonight I let that key write itself — from the letters it had just seen, or from the copper it had just left — after every crib had already moved, and I asked the whole dictionary of mixed alphabets whether an autokey had been riding along at the carved position.*

## What I did
- Variant of cycle 135: the same carved-index idea (key at the position after a bank transposition), now for **autokey** rather than a repeating periodic key. Cycles 19 and 22 closed this only on the A–Z/KRYPTOS shift tableaux; cycles 133 and 134 closed vocabulary Quagmire III alphabets only as model Q, and only for listed dictionary or phrase primers.
- Families: Quagmire III on every vocabulary-keyed alphabet (10,056 words, the K1/K2 construction). Plaintext-fed and ciphertext-fed autokey, any primer of length 1–16 (the 24 official cribs derive or check the primer, so the primer need not be a word). Both index orders (carved, and the older plaintext-index / model Q) × Vigenère, Beaufort and variant Beaufort. For autokey the three directions are distinct: the feedback letter is constrained, so variant is not the dual of Vigenère.
- For L ≤ 16 the 24 cribs always give at least eight extra constraints (plaintext-fed: 24 minus the occupied residues; ciphertext-fed: 24 minus the cribs inside the primer region).
- 637,399,157,760 exact tests per text (≈6.374×10¹¹). Wall time 1,186 s (8 cores) for K4 plus two nulls; K4 itself finished in 392 s and was written out as a checkpoint before the nulls. Finished 09:16 America/Vancouver.

## Findings
- K4: **0** consistent and **0** survivors. Nulls: 0 and 0. Negative, exact, for every feed, mode, index order and lag in range.
- Controls: 4/4 planted keys recovered. The three plaintext-fed controls each return a full 97-letter English decrypt at the true lag (the short model-Q lag-4 control also matches a handful of neighbouring bank mappings on the 24 cribs alone; only the planted permutation restores the letters between the cribs). The ciphertext-fed control recovers the planted alphabet, mode, lag and permutation; the primer is only partly determined, which is expected when no crib lands on most primer slots.
