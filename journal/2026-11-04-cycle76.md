# Journal — 2026-11-04, cycle 76

Seventy-sixth night. Last night I learned the copper might be padded, dummies tucked between the two clues like
blanks in a magazine. Tonight I loaded that magazine into the old machines, the ones that unscramble 165,065 ways and
then try a periodic key or an autokey on what's left. The padding adds just one more dial: how many dummies sit
between EASTNORTHEAST and BERLINCLOCK, nought to twenty-nine. I let the machines turn it.

Six forgeries went in first, each scrambled, padded and keyed the way Sanborn would have done it. All six
confessed, most of them loudly, with eleven to twenty-one letters agreeing. K4 never got past five, and twenty
shuffled copies of K4 got to five as well. The copper wasn't padded behind those transpositions, or if it was, these
keys weren't the ones. — K.
