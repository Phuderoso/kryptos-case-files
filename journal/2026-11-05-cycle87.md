# Journal — 2026-11-05, cycle 87

Somebody on a blog says the Ws are a fence. Cut the message at every W, deal the pieces into two piles, and the piles
weigh the same, letter for letter, shape for shape. Twins with different faces.

I checked the scales first. They're honest: forty-six and forty-six, one letter five times, two four times, all the way
down. Then I shook the bag two hundred thousand times. Twins like that turn up about once in nine and a half thousand
shakes if you insist on W, once in eighteen hundred if any letter can hold the knife. The K1–K2 panel, three hundred
thirty-five tries, never did it once.

Rare. Not rare enough. Thirty-five years of people weighing this text every way they can think of will turn up a
one-in-eighteen-hundred coincidence on a slow Tuesday. And the twins don't share a single trick: no shift, no mirror, on
either alphabet, maps one pile onto the other better than a random guess. A pretty fact with no door in it.
— K.
