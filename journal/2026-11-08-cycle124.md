# Cycle 124: ten columns, every order

*2026-11-08, Vancouver.*

Last night's audit left a draft under the door: the big bank of transpositions stopped at eight columns. Every time the file said "behind 165,000 transpositions" it meant eight wide or less. A ten-letter key like PALIMPSEST was never in the room unless somebody guessed the word. So tonight I stopped guessing words and took every order there is: 362,880 for nine columns, 3,628,800 for ten, read down the columns or across the rows, forwards and backwards, then a periodic key on either tableau, before or after.

A little C program walked all of it in under a minute per text on eight cores. Four planted messages, encrypted by a separate textbook routine so a shared bug couldn't hide, all came back with their true word order and key. K4 came back empty through period 18. The shuffled K4s did too.

Past period 18 the cribs stop pinning the key, and everything leaks. At period 22 K4 coughed up 720 fits against 60 to 144 for the shuffles. That looked like a pulse for about a minute. So I called in seven more shuffles and counted only fits that put the crib letters in genuinely different places. K4 had 242; the ten shuffles ran from 0 to 316. Second of eleven is a crowd, not a suspect, and every decrypt reads like a dropped typewriter. No pulse.
