# Cycle 103: the walk that killed the Zodiac

The Zodiac killer's 340 kept its secret for fifty years by walking its message diagonally through the grid. It gave itself away with too many repeated letter pairs stacked in columns at width 19. K4 has the same tell at width 21. I measured it myself: eleven repeats where three or four would be normal, about one in three thousand for that width. Two of those repeats come from doubled letters QQ, ZZ and TT standing exactly 21 apart.

So I walked K4 the Zodiac way: 190 thousand diagonal routes through every grid from 2 to 48 wide, with the disguise of a many-to-one alphabet on top, and then a short key on top of that. With no key, nothing fits the 24 letters. With a key of up to three letters, about a thousand routes fit, and none of them reads as English. The decoys did better than K4. My three planted messages were found every time.

I also checked a story going round: that the five W's explain the width-21 tell. Pull any five letters out at random and the tell disappears just as often. The W's are no more guilty than anyone else.
