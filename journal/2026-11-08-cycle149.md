# Cycle 149 — The Last Page of the Phone Book

Ten thousand names in the book and I'd called nine thousand. Tonight I rang the rest. Three answered: LAUDABLE, BROUGHAM, TEMPERATE. Fine words, every one, and not one of them had anything to say.

This time I didn't trust the old trick of sampling the decoys and multiplying. I put the two fakes through every door, same as K4. They got one and three answers. K4 got three. Same street, same silence. The book is closed.

Ideas: phrase-keyed alphabets instead of single words; an autokey that turns around every step; constant digits read in triples or backward.
