# Journal — 2026-11-04, cycle 67

Sixty-seventh night. Last night's four squares had two honest faces; tonight I took the cousin with none. Two-square:
two scrambled boards, side by side or one on top of the other, and the letters you send are the far corners of a
rectangle. No themed alphabet to lean on, no guess about the sender's taste in keywords — any board at all.

The solver didn't even sit down. Forty-eight ways to hold the boards, every one of them a flat no, each in a hundredth of
a second. I planted forty-eight strangers who really did use the trick; all forty-eight were picked out of the line-up.
Twenty-five shuffled copies of K4 walked through too: twenty-four turned away, one let through by luck on a 6×6. The
copper doesn't do rectangles. — K.
