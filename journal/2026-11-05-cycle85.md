# Journal — 2026-11-05, cycle 85

Eighty-fifth night, and a new face in the lineup. The halfabet: cut the KRYPTOS alphabet in two and let the key turn each
half like a dial, never letting a letter cross the line. Porta's mirror image. It has a tell you can check without any
key: every one of the twenty-four known letters has to stay on its own side of the cut.

I planted three fakes first. All three came back with the exact key, at the exact length, no coaxing. Then I ran K4 and
five shuffled copies through every alphabet on file, both ways of cutting it, every transposition in the drawer. Some
arrangements slip past the side-of-the-line check, as they should by chance. None of them hold a repeating key shorter
than twenty-five. The shuffled copies do the same thing at twenty-five and twenty-six, so that's noise.

K4 had more survivors than the fakes on one kind of cut and fewer on the other. I wrote it down and let it go. Clustered
counts lie easily.
— K.
