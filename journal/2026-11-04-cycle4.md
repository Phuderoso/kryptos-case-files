# Journal — 4 Nov 2026, cycle 4: the night shift

*The city had gone quiet the way only a cipher can — ninety-seven letters, staring back, saying nothing twice. I let the machine walk the alleys: swap two columns, taste the English, keep it if it's sweeter, sometimes keep it even if it isn't. Annealing, they call it. Cooling metal. Cooling suspects.*

## What I did (método)
1. **Quadgram model.** Built from five Project Gutenberg novels (2.94M quadgrams). The script is `tools/build_quadgrams.py`; the texts are downloaded, not committed.
2. **Annealer** (`tools/k4_anneal.py`). Transposition families: double columnar (widths 5–9 × 5–9) and Myszkowski (widths 5–12). Each is combined with a periodic Vigenère: P 1–7, AZ or KA alphabet, layer order B1 or B2.
   - The crib letters force the key at the constrained residues. The free residues take the best monogram fit.
   - Score = quadgram log-probability − 40 × crib conflicts.
   - 3,696 runs of 20,000 steps each, on 8 cores.
3. **Control.** I planted English with the cribs in position, encrypted it with double columnar (6,8) plus KA Vigenère "CLOCK", and ran it through the same annealer. The exact keys came back in 4 of 16 restarts. Output: `analysis/anneal_control.txt`.
4. **Weltzeituhr** (`tools/k4_cities.py`). City-name keyword alphabets and city-sequence running keys.
5. **Citations.** I replaced the 12 Wikipedia-routed citations with primary or archived links, text-checking each one where it isn't paywalled.

## Findings (fatos)
- **Annealer.** Zero runs out of 3,696 satisfy all 24 crib letters; the best still has 8 conflicts. The best quadgram score is −7.00/char, against −8.08 for random text and ≈ −4.2 for English.
- **City names.** None of the 39,260 city-alphabet tests survives once at least 6 constraints are required. The best running key matches 6 of 24 crib letters, which is chance level.
- **Pre-1997 city list.** I could not reconstruct it. Only 5 of the 20 cities added in 1997 and 4 renames are documented.

## Caveats (honestidade)
Annealing is a heuristic. The control suggests roughly a 68% chance of detection per configuration at these widths. The result says "very unlikely", not "impossible".

## Next cycle (proposal)
- More restarts and wider widths (10–14) for the families that came closest.
- Transposition families with irregular grids: K3-style rotations at all dimensions combined with substitution, and disrupted columnar.
- A non-periodic substitution (autokey) inside the annealer.
- Hunt for 1969–1996 photographs and postcards (Bild und Heimat) to complete the city list.
