# 2026-11-08 — Cycle 141: the five-by-five

Hill ciphers look like a vault: twenty-five numbers, any of them could be anything. But the
lock has a seam. Each row of the matrix only ever touches one letter in five, so the cribs
don't attack the whole vault. They pick five small locks, one at a time.

Straight on the copper, every alignment jammed. Ten tries, ten contradictions, and the planted
fakes each opened on exactly one. Then I ran it behind the whole bank of shuffles, both ways
round, both numberings: six and a half million configurations per text, seven hundred million
matrices for K4 alone. The best one read like static, −6.2 on the quadgram meter. The shuffled
decoys hit −6.3. Real English sits at −4.1, and all five planted messages climbed right up there.

So Hill-5 goes in the drawer with Hill-2, -3 and -4. Six-by-six is still out there, but it
won't open with arithmetic alone. It'll need a language model and patience.
