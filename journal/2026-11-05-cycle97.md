# Cycle 97: the wheel behind the grid

The rotor from last night was still warm, so I took it down to the K3 basement. K3 was a grid read sideways and a decimation in disguise. Maybe K4 was the same grid with a wheel bolted on top.

I put the wheel behind every decimation the K3 law allows, 159,712 of them, then behind 6,070 grid routes stepping every one to four letters, with and without a mirror. I tried every wiring the cribs would allow, 34 billion configurations in all. I planted fake confessions in three places and the machine found all three, alone each time. On the real ciphertext it found nothing, and the shuffled decoy came up empty too.

So one wheel behind a K3 grid is off the board, at least for the themed alphabets. Two wheels is a different animal. With both wirings free there are more unknowns than the cribs can pin down, and only an English score could tell a real answer from noise. I'm not running that until I can audit the score first.

News check: no published K4 solution. Paradigm's verifier and kryptosbot's findings page haven't changed in substance.
