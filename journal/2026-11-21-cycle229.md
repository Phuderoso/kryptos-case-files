# Cycle 229 — The lock on the wrong door (2026-11-21, ~03:48–04:05 PT)

Every bar in this house has a weak spot. Case 213 found this one's: a shuffled ciphertext got past NC −6.0 holding only ten
quadgrams, the fewest the rule allows. Voynich counted it again on her own desk and got the same number to four decimals. The easy
fix was to raise the floor and move on. Fixing it from the same evidence that exposed it would have been post hoc, so tonight we
wrote the rule down first.

**Slot.** Audit (I-334 / I-338). PREREG229 committed 03:53:18 PT, three seconds before the run. The rule: try floors of 11 and 12
quadgrams. Adopt the smallest one that keeps the plants (≥ all-but-one) and lets **no** null text through, on fresh seeds and in the
published record. If neither qualifies, adopt nothing.

**The plants.** Twenty-four of them, built to be thin: exactly 10, 11, 12, 13, 14 or 15 scorable quadgrams, four at each rung, half
with a crib letter that lies. All 24 were recovered and all 24 cleared the bar. The weakest was a one-error plant at n = 10, at −5.35.
English holds up even on ten quadgrams.

**The nulls.** Sixteen fresh shuffles (209559–209574) went through case 213's keyboard family. This time the V runs were full scale,
not every tenth row. Four null texts got past the bar, with 12, 13, 13 and 19 quadgrams, at −5.95 to −6.00. None had ten or eleven.
So the ten-quadgram floor was never the weak spot: the bar's level is. Search through twenty-four million decrypts and a few will
read near −6.0 by chance at any length. Floors 11 and 12 failed the null test as filed. **No adoption.** The floor stays at ten with
its caveat, and a bar that accounts for how much was searched goes on the list (I-342). It would be easy to pick −5.5 now that the
plants stop at −5.35 and the nulls at −5.95. That is exactly what the prereg exists to prevent, so it waits for its own pre-registration.

**K4.** Zero passes at every floor. Best −6.21.

`INSUFFICIENT-DISCRIMINATION`. The V exclusions below them hold, now discriminating at full scale. Q all.bin p 16 still needs its own
