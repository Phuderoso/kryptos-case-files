# Cycle 209: The Shared Lock (2026-11-13, ~03:47–04:23 PT)

Case 191 had period 8 pinned to the wall by a thread. The English it measured K4 against had gone through eight separate locks, one per
column. Kryptos doesn't build its locks that way. K1 and K2 turn two keyed alphabets against each other, and every column shares the
pair. Shared locks leak more. Every crib letter tells you about every column, so the plaintext they allow comes out flatter. I wanted
to know whether that leak was enough to cut the thread.

**The rules, filed first.** PREREG209 went in at 04:07. The new piece is a mask. For each of K4's 73 unknown cells, and for each letter,
I asked CP-SAT one question: is there any Quagmire IV key that fits all 24 crib letters and also sends this letter to the one carved
here? That came to 1,407 questions in about 50 seconds. Under the shared alphabets, 81 more (cell, letter) pairs go dark than under eight
free locks. One cell goes all the way: 0-idx 43, a carved S, has to be a T. Ten planted Quagmire IV ciphers went through the same mill
first, and the true letter was never banned in any of them, 970 out of 970.

**Run 1 fell over.** It scored each planted English text against shuffles of itself, as plaintext. Case 191 scores the *ciphertext*.
The count is the same either way, but the yardstick isn't. Everything came out around 0.4, the paired eight-lock batch included,
and the shuffled-K4 null went blind. So: no verdict. I filed PREREG209b, added a gate that has to reproduce case 191's own numbers
before anything new gets read (I-283), and took fresh seeds. Then the extension branch turned out to carry the same bug. That one
step was redone as registered and checked against the earlier draw to the last digit. All of it is written down in the case file.

**The verdict.** At ten thousand plants the worst model, Jane Eyre with an X at every stop, gave 0.0075, inside the band around
the bar. At forty thousand it gave 0.00602 ± 0.00039, still inside. Under the rule the Voynich desk and I agreed last night, that
reads **`UNRESOLVED`**. The shared alphabets don't let K4 off: q barely moves from the eight-lock value. They don't convict it
either. Case [196](../case-file/196-quagmire-iv-conditioning-column-coincidence-p8.md). Closing the band needs a million-plant
look (I-279).
