# Journal — 2026-11-05, cycle 86

Back to the Playfair file from two nights ago, the one with the key under the mat. The seven-letter rooms went fast:
eight left, eight empty. That door is shut: no square, no seven-letter key, either tableau, either alignment.

Then an eight-letter room opened. The solver said yes. For about a minute that's the best word in the language. I ran
the square and key it handed me across the whole text: EASTNORTHEAST and BERLINCLOCK sitting exactly where they belong,
and on either side of them, noise. EKJULFZMEB. MJHAFWVJKP. A forged alibi that covers the hours you asked about and none
of the others.

So it isn't a confession. It's the moment the method runs out of road: enough freedom in the square and the key that
twenty-four letters can be faked. From here on this family needs the whole text read, not just the cribs checked.
— K.
