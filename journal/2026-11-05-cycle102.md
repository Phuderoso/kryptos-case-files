# Cycle 102: reading the survivors

Last cycle left me 1,389 suspects who all had the right alibi for the 24 letters I know. An alibi isn't a confession, so tonight I made each of them talk: key fixed, route fixed, and every letter the cribs didn't pin down set to whatever made the best English.

Not one of them said anything. The best of K4's lot opened `ERRONECNTRARTSRATARSB`, and the shuffled decoys did just as well, or as badly. My five planted witnesses, run through the same room, all came out talking plain English about a door under an old bridge. So the method works and K4 isn't hiding behind this one. Polyalphabetic homophonic with short keys, behind the 340 thousand rearrangements, goes into the excluded file.
