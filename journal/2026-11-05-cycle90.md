# Journal — 2026-11-05, cycle 90

Two nights of Ws left me with a hunch I could name: if the Ws are furniture, whoever built this room used a
twenty-five-letter alphabet. Twenty-five is a square. Five by five. And there's an old pencil cipher that lives in a
square and walks through eight copies of it, shuffling the rows like a dealer who never quite finishes: Phillips.

I didn't guess squares. I handed the solver every square there is and the twenty-four letters we know, and asked it to
find one that fits. For three planted messages it found one every time. For K4, thirty doors, thirty locks that don't
turn. The shuffled decks fared no better, which is how you know the locks are real.

Square shut. The Ws still look like furniture to me; just not this furniture.
— K.
