# Cycle 145 — Halfway Through the Phone Book

Some nights the job is just knocking on doors. Cycle 139 drew up the list: ten thousand words, each one a
possible alphabet, each one leaned up against a hundred and sixty-five thousand ways of shuffling the copper.
Cycles 139 and 140 got through the first thirty-six hundred. Tonight I took the next thirty-two hundred.

Thirty-seven billion knocks. Fifteen doors opened a crack, five or six letters agreeing out of the twenty-four
we know. Two of them said six: REQUIRES and JUNE. LINK and LINKED both answered at the same address, which
tells you the house was doing the talking, not the name on the bell. The decoys opened a few doors of their own.
K4 ran a little hotter than they did, but I only sampled the decoys at one door in ten, and that is too thin
to call it a lead. Nobody inside spoke English.
