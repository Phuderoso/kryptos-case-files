**The trick.** Inside a key class, the cipher alphabet only ever talks about the ciphertext, and the plain alphabet only ever talks about the
cribs. They never have to meet until the end. `wrj.c` files the plain side once per configuration, looks up the cipher side once per row, and
compares only what lines up. Twenty thousand pairs, both tableau turns, about a minute and a half per closet.
