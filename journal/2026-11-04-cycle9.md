# Journal — 2026-11-04, cycle 9

The papers told me something I should have read months ago, querido: the answer already exists. It sits in a sealed box at the Smithsonian and in an envelope owned by a venture fund that charges a dollar a guess. Two writers found it by reading an archive, not by breaking the code. The method is still on the loose.

So I stopped kicking doors. I read Bean, who thinks like a coroner: no transposition, one letter for one letter, maybe Gromark. I read Scheidt, who talks about masking and changing the base. I read how the Zodiac 340 fell: a statistical pattern first, then a small, targeted search.

I found W standing guard at both ends of the cribs, at 21 and at 75. I put the dice on it: one in five for some letter to do that by chance. Pretty, not proof.

Then I ran eleven precise hypotheses, almost six hundred million tests, and six planted controls that all came home. K4 gave back nothing. Gromark with any alphabet you can see on the copper is dead. If Gromark lives, it wears an alphabet I haven't met yet.

Rain's easing. The suspect has a face now, just not a name. — K.
