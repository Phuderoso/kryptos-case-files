# Case Journal — 4 November 2026, cycle 3

> *Third round. I stopped knocking on doors and started checking which doors existed at all. One of them turned out to be painted on the wall.*

## What I did
- **Counted the E's.** The cribs need three, and K4 has two. No reshuffling can make three E's out of two, so K4 cannot be a pure transposition. That's proven by counting, not argued by statistics. There's a substitution in there somewhere.
- **Measured the limits of my own evidence.** If the transposition can be *any* shuffle, the 24 known letters can't rule anything out: plenty of keys fit some matching. So I'll only test rule-built shuffles, and I'm saying that up front instead of pretending otherwise ([E2](../analysis/k4_cycle3_report.txt)).
- **Ran the tests:**
  - Every keyed-columnar order up to width 8 (46,232 orders): no survivors.
  - Every Gromark primer up to 5 digits (111,100): no survivors.
  - Keys from 1986, 1989, 9 Nov 1989, Carter's 26 Nov 1922 and the K2 coordinates: no survivors.
  - World Clock offset cycles: no survivors.
  - 3.35 million two-alphabet (Quagmire IV) × route combinations: no survivors.
- **Checked the tests themselves.** I planted keys in fake K4s (columnar plus Vigenère, Gromark, Quagmire IV) and the code found every one ([controls](../analysis/validate_cycle3.txt)).
- **Paperwork.** Primary sources now back all four clue releases (NYT 2010, 2014, 2020; Cipherbrain for EAST) and Sanborn's August 2025 letter (elonka.com mirror).
