# Cycle 93 — The square with no W

*Nov 5, 2026, ~03:40 Vancouver time*

The rule said new territory tonight, so I took the five Ws off the wall and looked at what was left: 92 letters, 25
of them, the exact size of a 5×5 square. Delastelle's Bifid lives in squares like that. Cycle 43 had cleared the 6×6
room; nobody had knocked on the 5×5 door with the Ws gone.

The solver went through every arrangement of the square at every period, both directions, in under a minute. One
square: **zero hits in 184**. Shuffled decoys: zero too. Planted controls lit up at their true period and nowhere
else. **W-null + classic Bifid is excluded** ([case file 88](../case-file/88-bifid-w-free.md)).

With a second square for the output (Bifid plus a relettering), K4 slipped through at two periods, 28 and 30 — fewer
than any of the five shuffled decoys (3–11). That isn't a lead; the cribs just can't hold that model. I put an
annealer on the two survivors anyway, and planted a control first. The control beat it: twice, with 40 and then 16
long restarts, it never found its own plaintext. So the K4 runs (gibberish, at decoy level) prove nothing either way.
The two-square case is filed **open**, with a sharper method queued.
