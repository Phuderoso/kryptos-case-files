# 2026-11-08 — Cycle 140: three thousand more doors

The machine from last night was still warm, so I fed it more keys. Three thousand new words,
each one cut into a crooked alphabet, each one walked past every one of the 330,130 shuffles in
the bank. Thirty-four billion questions in six minutes, and the only answers that came back were
the kind a shuffled deck gives you too: five matching links, sometimes six, then nothing.

Ten hits for K4. The two fake K4s, sampled at a tenth, coughed up three between them, which
scales to about what K4 managed. TEMPER, EAT, COMPASSION, CHRISTMAS: none of them read past the
cribs. The sculpture didn't flinch.

I also went back over cycle 138's notes. I'd counted odd cycles there as if they were real
constraints. They're coin flips. The verdict stands, but the fine print got rewritten.

Six thousand five hundred words still wait in the queue. Next cycle needs a new idea anyway.
