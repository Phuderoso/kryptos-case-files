## Why this case exists (audit, not a new search)
[Case 193](193-word-restart-two-alphabets-and-behind-bank.md) found **0** K4 survivors for the word-restart key R1 behind the
165,065-entry transposition bank at L 1–17. The Voynich desk (box-local audit, plan sha prefixes 0b7210b2 / bb709c65; claims checked
below where re-derivable) then asked whether the **planted controls** actually covered the hard cases:

- A *transposition block* is a written row of the family grid of width w; a crib word boundary at layer index b is **mid-block** iff
  b % w ≠ 0. 87.2 % of (entry, segmentation) pairs are mid-block; 0.30 % are **grid-aligned** with an internal boundary.
- Case 193's plants were 2 (L 8 and L 10), both mid-block, **none at L 16 / 17 and none aligned**.

So the L ≤ 16 zero was an exact full-bank count, but not a plant-validated one at the frontier, and the aligned minority was never
planted. A bug that only bit aligned word splits or long keys would not have been seen. This cycle closes that control gap.

## Design (as registered)
- **Stratum M (mid-block), 20 plants, L 16:** invented text with words EAST | NORTHEAST and BERLIN | CLOCK (boundaries 25, 69:
  mid-block for every one of the 165,064 non-identity entries). Entry uniform over those.
- **Stratum A (grid-aligned), 20 plants, L 16:** same text with EASTNORTH | EAST and BERLINCLOCK (boundary 30). Entry uniform over the
  **3,138** non-identity entries whose every component width divides 30 (the desk counted 3,139 with identity included, consistent).
- Tableau/form cycles AZ Vig, AZ Beau, KA Vig, KA Beau (5 each), direction uniform, key 16 uniform random letters; seed 206207 (the
  seed the desk reserved). Optional: 5 mid-block plants at L 17, threshold 5 / 5, descriptive.
- **Recovered** iff the full-bank run at that L prints a survivor at the true (entry, direction, tableau/form, L, segmentation,
  offsets 0 / 0) with the planted key on every determined index. **Gate:** ≥ 19 / 20 in **each** L 16 stratum (exact power 1 / 0.983 /
  0.736 at per-plant detection p = 1 / 0.99 / 0.95).

## What this does and does not show
- **Shows:** within the registered scope (R1 word restart, model Q, this bank, AZ/KA × Vig/Beau/variant, case 190 segmentations,
  offsets 0 here), `wrbank` recovers planted ciphers at the clean-exclusion frontier L 16, on both mid-block and grid-aligned word
  splits, at ≥ 19 / 20 each. Case 193's L ≤ 16 `EXCLUDED` may now be cited as **plant-validated at L 16 on both strata**.
- **Does not show:** the plants were generated by this desk's own encoder, so a shared convention error between encoder and solver
  would pass silently; the independent check of the *convention* is the desk's separate recount of K4 (0 / 4 / 106 at L 17 / 18 / 19,
  doubled convention), not this gate. Non-zero first-word offsets were not planted at L 16 (they were covered only by the full count).
  A pass at p = 1 is consistent with, not proof of, zero misses on every cell type.
- **K4 is not reopened**; no K4 count was run this cycle.
