# Case file 129 — Hill 5×5, direct and behind the bank

**Cycle 141, 2026-11-08. New family extension: Hill n = 5 (previously open; n ≤ 4 closed in cycles 27 and 29).**

## Idea

A Hill cipher is the cleanest "layered simple" machine there is: one matrix, applied block by
block. Its weakness for an attacker with cribs is that every row of the decryption matrix is
independent: row r produces only plaintext positions ≡ o + r (mod 5). So the 24 crib letters
split into five small linear systems, one per row. Directly, they give 4, 5, 5, 6 and 4
equations (in some order, depending on alignment) for 5 unknowns each. That means the matrix is
almost pinned down, and one row carries a real check, so the direct case is an exact test.
Behind a transposition the crib letters scatter and the systems vary; where they are
underdetermined the remaining freedom is small enough to enumerate and rank by English.

## What was tested

- Direct: 5 alignments × 2 numberings (A–Z, KRYPTOS alphabet).
- Behind 330,130 bank permutations (165,065 entries both ways) × model Q and carved order ×
  5 alignments × 2 numberings: 6.6 million configurations per text, about 140 s on 8 cores.
- Solving is exact mod 2 and mod 13 (CRT to mod 26), matrices must be invertible mod 26.
- Ranking: mean log10 quadgram over windows outside the cribs. Planted English scores about
  −4.1; shuffled text about −7.

## Results

| Text | Inconsistent | Underdetermined (skipped) | Scored | Best mean quadgram |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| K4 | 6,429,922 | 38,868 | 133,810 | −6.17 |
| null 1 | 6,423,456 | 44,322 | 134,822 | −6.29 |
| null 2 | 6,482,122 | 25,346 | 95,132 | −6.29 |

Direct: all 10 configurations are inconsistent for K4 and both nulls (each control has exactly
its true configuration consistent). Controls: 5/5 recovered with readable plaintext at −4.06
to −4.12 (two direct, one model Q, two carved; one carved control needed the larger caps).

K4's best is 2 log-units below English and inside the null range; its score histogram matches
the nulls'. **Negative**: no Hill 5×5, direct or behind any bank transposition in these orders,
produces English.

## Limits

- 0.6 % of bank configurations (38,868 for K4) were too underdetermined for the default caps
  (a row with ≤ 3 equations, or > 200,000 matrices) and were skipped.
- Only standard and KRYPTOS numberings; arbitrary numbering (as cycle 29 did for 2×2) is open.
- Hill n ≥ 6 stays open: directly each row gets about 4 equations for 6 unknowns, so it
  needs annealing rather than enumeration.
- The two-letter tail (or head) outside full blocks is left undecoded.
