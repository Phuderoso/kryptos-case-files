# Case file 133 — PT-autokey with a free plain alphabet, behind the bank, alphabets 3601–6800

**Cycle 145, 2026-11-08. Variant of cycles 139–140 (case files 127–128).**

## What was tested

Same model as case files 127–128: plaintext-fed autokey tested only on crib-to-crib links, so the
primer and every unknown plaintext letter drop out and only the cipher alphabet, the free
plaintext alphabet and the lag remain. Key letter read through the cipher alphabet (y), plain
A–Z, the KRYPTOS alphabet, or the free plain alphabet (x). Vigenère, Beaufort-style and variant
forms; every lag giving at least 5 crib-to-crib checks.

This cycle covers the next 3,200 vocabulary alphabets (words 3601–6800 of `all.txt`), behind all
165,065 bank entries used both ways (330,130 permutations), carved order and model Q.

- K4: 3.71 × 10¹⁰ exact tests on 8 cores.
- Nulls (cycle 139's two shuffled K4s): every 10th bank entry, 7.4 × 10⁹ tests.
- Total wall time 9 min 29 s (over the 8-minute budget; the last slice is split in two).

## Result

| Text | Bank share | Hits (lines) | Distinct configurations |
|---|---|---|---|
| K4 | full | 30 | 15 (each a bank duplicate pair q, q+1) |
| null 1 | 1/10 | 1 | 1 |
| null 2 | 1/10 | 2 | 2 |

Scaled the same way as case file 128 (a 1/10 sample sees a duplicated configuration about 2 times
in 10), the nulls expect roughly 5 and 10 distinct configurations on the full bank. K4's 15 is at
the top of that range or a little above it, but the null estimate rests on only 1–2 sampled hits
each, so its uncertainty is wide. Across cycles 139–145 K4 has not produced a single
configuration with more than 6 checks. Thirteen K4 configurations sit at the 5-check minimum; two
reach 6 (REQUIRES with a KRYPTOS key alphabet, plaintext order, lag 3; JUNE with the key read
through the free plain alphabet, difference form, carved, lag 12, 28.2 bits). LINK and LINKED hit
the same bank entry at lag 25, which suggests the hit comes from the transposition's crib
geometry rather than the word. Survivor list: `data/cycle145/survivors.txt`.

**Negative, with a caveat.** Nothing reaches the strength a real key would show (a planted control
lands well above threshold), but the null baseline for this run is thin.

## Limits and next steps

- Words 6801–10,056 behind the bank remain open (about 9.5 minutes; split across two cycles).
- Firm up the baseline: run one null on the full bank for a 400-word slice, so the K4-to-null
  comparison does not lean on a 1/10 sample.
- The two 6-check configurations could be decrypted with a language model scoring the free plain
  alphabet; at 5–6 checks they are underdetermined, so expect gibberish.
